## Research Interests

**Modeling, Optimization, and Control of Complex Aerospace Systems**
Focusing on structural dynamics, multi-objective optimization, and intelligent control systems. This research integrates CAD, FEA, and optimization methods to advance high-fidelity modeling, adaptive control, and simulation-based design in aircraft systems.

**Interdisciplinary System Optimization**
Transfer of aerospace system modeling techniques to biomedical and motion science applications. Topics include martial arts biomechanics, human motion dynamics, and complex medical-human system optimization.
