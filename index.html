Fan Shen

Associate Researcher, School of System Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China

shenfan@mail.sysu.edu.cnGitHubORCID

I work on modeling, simulation and model credibility for complex systems. I trained in aerospace at Harbin Institute of Technology and worked with Rolls-Royce at Queen's University Belfast on whole-engine structural design, where decisions rest on simulations that cannot be fully tested.

I now bring the same practice, integrating models from many sources and grading how far each can be trusted, to individual health and behavioral decisions, where published findings rarely become models anyone can run.

Research

  • Life Matters. An open framework and model library that turns published health and behavioral findings into runnable, composable models for individual decisions.
  • Structural Topology Optimisation. Topology optimisation of gas turbine engine and nacelle components with Rolls-Royce partners.
  • Solar Sail Deformation and Adjustment. Deformation modeling and trajectory adjustment for flexible solar sail structures.
