Fan Shen

Associate Researcher, School of System Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China

shenfan@mail.sysu.edu.cnGitHubORCID

I work on systems modelling and simulation, with a focus on component-based dynamical modelling and the behaviour of the whole system once its component models are joined. My current work turns published findings on individual health and behaviour into models anyone can run, simulate and check, because in this field such findings rarely become runnable models.

Before this I spent ten years on aircraft and spacecraft. At Queen's University Belfast I worked with Rolls-Royce on whole-engine structural design, where decisions rest on simulations that cannot be fully tested. My doctoral work at Harbin Institute of Technology modelled the dynamics and control of flexible solar sails.

Life Matters

An open framework that turns published health and behavioural findings into runnable, composable models. Each model names its sources, and an engine simulates the models and searches for the trade-offs when goals conflict.
