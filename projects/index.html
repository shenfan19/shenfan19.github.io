Life Matters

An open framework that turns published health and behavioural findings into runnable, composable models for individual decisions. It has two parts.

  • Model library. Models as plain files that name their sources, one per published finding or mechanism.
  • Reference Engine. The engine that runs the models, composes them and searches for trade-offs when goals conflict.

Built on Life Matters

Browser games whose characters live by the Life Matters models. They are for entertainment only, not a source for health or medical advice.

  • Care for You. A small browser game about looking after the people at home, built as a hands-on front end for the Life Matters models.
  • The Past Is Yet to Come. A narrative browser game in which you run a time-travel agency, send employees to turning points in history and read the reports they bring back.
